Our Strength

Inspiring worship service

— “This is a place we believe is worth coming to—and worth bringing others to.”

Many of us come ready to participate, and we can see something in our worship gatherings that matters. There is a sense that what happens here can connect with real life and help us turn toward God. Perhaps most encouragingly, we believe these gatherings can also be a place where people who are not yet part of church life might come and find something meaningful.

That does not mean every gathering reaches every one of us in the same way. Some of us do not always look forward to coming, and there are times when worship can leave us less engaged than we would hope. But underneath that tension is something we value: gathering together still has purpose for us, and we want to be part of it rather than simply watch from the edges.

Thank you for giving us a shared place where we can come, participate, encounter God and welcome others into something we ourselves value.

Our Struggle

Effective structures

— “We want to contribute, but we don’t always know how all our efforts fit together.”

There is willingness and ability among us. Many of us know something of what God has given us, can see value in what we contribute, and experience God using it. Yet it can be difficult to see where we are going together or how the different parts of our church connect. Planning, coordination and shared responsibility do not always give us the clarity and support we need to offer what we have with confidence.

That can leave a gap between wanting to contribute and experiencing ourselves as part of a genuinely shared endeavour. We do not simply want more things to do. We want to know that what each of us brings belongs within something we understand and can participate in together.

Please help us experience church life as something we share—not just through the tasks we each perform, but through clearer purpose, stronger connection and the sense that we really are carrying it together.
